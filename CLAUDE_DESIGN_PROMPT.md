# Claude Design Prompt — Phase 1 Homepage / Design System

Copy this prompt into Claude Design or Claude Code when starting the first visual concept.

---

You are designing the first high-end frontend concept for a Serbian automotive refinish distributor website: **Carsystem i R-M Inđija d.o.o.**

This is not a basic catalog website. The goal is to create a premium, high-tech, automotive B2B showcase website that can also become a real B2B platform later.

## Core Goal

Create a visually exceptional homepage and design system direction that communicates:

- serious wholesale/distribution company,
- professional automotive refinish ecosystem,
- official long-term brand credibility,
- strong partner store network in Serbia,
- premium technical support,
- modern digital-first company,
- Studio One-level portfolio quality.

## Primary Business Objective

The most important CTA is not “buy now”. The most important CTA is:

**“Pronađite najbližu prodavnicu”**

The website should help end customers find the nearest partner store in Serbia. The partner network is central because if partner stores sell more, the company grows more.

Create a subtle sticky/floating locator CTA that feels premium, not spammy. It can be a side card, floating widget, or compact CTA module. It should invite the user to allow location or manually choose a city/region.

## Audience

Design for:

1. end customers who need to find products and nearest store,
2. partner stores and potential partners,
3. auto-lakirnice, limarske radionice and servisi,
4. B2B buyers who will later log in to see private prices,
5. business owners who may view this as a Studio One case study.

## Brand / Product Ecosystem

Use placeholders or neutral treatments for these brand names unless official assets are provided:

- Carsystem
- R-M
- Baslac
- Norbin
- Befar
- Cosmos Spray
- SATA
- Rupes
- BigFoot
- Car Fit

Do not invent legal claims that are not in the copy. Use safe copy such as “profesionalni refinish program”, “brendovi u ponudi”, “partnerska mreža”, “tehnička podrška”.

## Visual Style

Create an ultra-modern automotive/refinish look:

- premium,
- high-tech,
- precise,
- industrial but clean,
- dark/light theme capable,
- automotive surfaces,
- paint/reflection/gloss details,
- subtle gradients,
- glass-like panels only where useful,
- strong typography,
- polished spacing,
- no cheap template look.

The color direction should be inspired by R-M and Carsystem palettes, but slightly lean toward Carsystem’s stronger technical/automotive identity. Avoid overusing brand colors. Use them as accents.

## Hero Concept

Create a hero that feels like a scroll-trigger story of automotive refinishing.

Concept:
A premium visual sequence around painting/preparing a car bumper or car panel. The page should imply phases such as preparation, masking, sanding, primer, base coat, clear coat, polishing and final result.

For this first design phase, it does not need to implement a real video/canvas sequence. It can be represented as:

- layered hero composition,
- 3D-like automotive panel/bumper visual,
- timeline/progress phase indicators,
- motion-ready sections,
- frame cards suggesting the process,
- placeholders for future AI/video/image sequence.

Important: design must have mobile fallback in mind. Do not rely only on heavy desktop animation.

## Required Homepage Sections

Create these sections:

1. Hero
   - premium high-tech automotive visual,
   - headline in Serbian latinica,
   - short positioning text,
   - primary CTA: “Pronađite najbližu prodavnicu”,
   - secondary CTA: “Pogledaj katalog” or “Postani partner”.

2. Partner Store Locator Preview
   - location permission concept,
   - manual city/region fallback,
   - nearest partner card preview,
   - map/list preview.

3. Brand Ecosystem Strip
   - elegant display of brand names/logos/placeholders,
   - not too loud,
   - feels like professional supplier ecosystem.

4. Catalog Preview
   - product categories preview,
   - logged-out state: “Pošalji upit”,
   - future logged-in state: “Cene dostupne nakon prijave”.

5. Why Carsystem i R-M
   - veleprodaja,
   - partner network,
   - technical support,
   - color mixing/mikseri boja,
   - experienced sales reps,
   - Serbia coverage.

6. Partner-Focused Section
   - explain that partner stores are the center of the sales network,
   - CTA: “Postani partner” or “Kontaktiraj veleprodaju”.

7. Technical Support Section
   - support for lakirnice, servisi, prodavnice,
   - troubleshooting and product guidance,
   - professional tone.

8. Future B2B Login Preview
   - private prices,
   - customer discounts,
   - inquiry system,
   - sales rep support.
   - This should look like a premium dashboard preview, but do not build backend logic.

9. Footer
   - contact/location: Inđija, Serbia,
   - navigation,
   - partner/store locator CTA,
   - discreet “Design & development: Studio One” placeholder.

## Copy Tone

Language: Serbian latinica only.

Tone:

- professional,
- confident,
- premium,
- understandable to end customers,
- credible to B2B buyers,
- not too corporate,
- not cheesy sales copy.

Suggested headline directions:

- “Profesionalni refinish sistem za Srbiju.”
- “Boje, oprema i podrška za savršen završni sloj.”
- “Povezujemo kupce, prodavnice i profesionalne refinish brendove.”

You may improve these.

## Technical Constraints

Do not introduce paid monthly tools.

Prefer:

- Next.js compatible structure,
- Tailwind-friendly classes/design tokens,
- CSS/JS animation-ready approach,
- open-source libraries only,
- no dependency on paid map/search/CMS services.

Use placeholders for product images, brand logos and 3D visuals if needed.

## Output Expected

Produce a polished homepage concept with:

- desktop layout,
- mobile layout considerations,
- section-by-section structure,
- visual hierarchy,
- component naming suggestions,
- design tokens for colors/typography/spacing,
- animation notes,
- implementation notes for later Next.js build.

Prioritize wow-factor, but keep it realistic and maintainable.
