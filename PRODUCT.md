# Carsystem Product Context

## Product

Carsystem is the public website and future B2B platform for Carsystem i R-M Inđija, an automotive refinish distributor in Serbia. The current phase focuses on a premium public product catalog, brand presentation, partner-store routing, technical support positioning, and a clear path toward later authenticated B2B pricing and ordering.

## Primary users

- Professional body shops, painters, technicians, and purchasing staff who need to identify the right system, product, shade, code, and package.
- Partner stores and sales representatives who need a reliable public catalog to route and support customers.
- End customers who need understandable product guidance and a nearby authorized point of sale.

## Core jobs

- Browse and search a credible, technically structured product catalog.
- Filter products by brand, Carsystem workflow phase, product line, technical category, finish, code, and color.
- Understand what a product is for without exposing private B2B prices or inventory.
- Continue to an inquiry or partner-store route when a product is relevant.

## Brand and experience

The experience should feel professional, confident, premium, precise, and high-tech automotive. Serbian public copy uses Latin script and stays clear to non-experts while remaining credible to professionals.

## Anti-references

- Generic marketplace or commodity ecommerce themes.
- Cheesy, exaggerated, or unsupported marketing claims.
- Decorative motion that competes with product identification.
- Public prices, stock claims, or distribution claims that have not been verified.
- Paid SaaS dependencies where a maintainable self-owned solution is sufficient.

## Accessibility and platform requirements

- Excellent desktop, tablet, and mobile behavior.
- Keyboard-visible controls and robust focus states.
- Light and dark themes with readable contrast.
- Reduced-motion fallbacks for every non-essential animation.
- Lazy, dimensionally stable product media and cost-conscious static delivery.
